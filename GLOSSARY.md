# ha-spark

Language for the household energy controller and its Home Assistant companion.

## Language

**Fallback charging schedule**:
The owner's fixed charging times and limits used when automatic planning is unavailable or manual control expires. It is not an optimised plan.

**Operating mode**:
The owner's selected level of ha-spark control: Observe, Protect, or Optimise.

**Agent-editable setting**:
A household setting the owner has allowed the product agent to request changing. That permission does not itself approve a particular change.
