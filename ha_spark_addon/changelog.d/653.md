- **Native SoC for integration-owned sites (#653, #495).** Once the
  integration owns the site, the add-on's entry preconditions take SoC and its
  trust from the integration snapshot instead of the configured SoC entity,
  and no longer check the legacy Solis write entities. A missing, stale,
  future-dated or untrusted snapshot SoC fails Optimise entry with its reason;
  there is no fallback to the SoC entity. While the snapshot is live, the mode
  page still leaves entry checks to the integration. `ha-spark health` adds a
  **Native SoC** row and stops warning about the SoC, battery voltage, charge
  current and power switch entities on those sites. Add-on-owned sites are
  unchanged.
