- **Axle event history and estimated earnings (#613).** ha-spark keeps accepted
  export events and their rates on both add-on-owned and integration-owned
  sites. `ha-spark stats` reports estimated daily Axle earnings and the event
  delivery rate. Earnings credit actual cost and battery savings without
  changing the no-battery cost. A cancelled event earns no credit; a gap in
  measured export leaves the event without an estimate and its slots unpriced.
  These estimates are not Axle settlement figures.
