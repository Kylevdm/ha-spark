- **Integration supply guard in Observe (#650, #560).** On an integration-owned
  site in Observe the guard now shows the limit it would apply, marked
  `simulated` on its entities and in diagnostics, clears it when the import
  drops, and never latches or saves a cut; leaving Observe starts it clean.
  Its warn reasons are now distinct: `authority_unavailable` only for a
  missing or stale authority mirror, `mode_observe` in Observe or a pending
  transition to Observe, and `writes_not_permitted` for any other state that
  permits no writes, such as a failed save. Protect and Optimise are
  unchanged.
