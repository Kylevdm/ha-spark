- **Carry over a lowered discharge ceiling (#657, #561).** `ha-spark health`
  warns when a native inverter's effective DC discharge limit, converted using
  its bank's nominal voltage, exceeds an explicitly set
  `battery_discharge_ceiling_kw`. The read-only warning uses the durable accepted
  topology and never blocks control. The topology guide explains how to carry
  the lower ceiling over as a DC current limit, rounded down.
