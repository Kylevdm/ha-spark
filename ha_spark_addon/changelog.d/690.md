- **Free Electricity Sessions are read (#690).** ha-spark now reads the Octopus Free Electricity Sessions listed for your account from the public Octopus API, read-only, and counts them only when the account is enrolled in the campaign. `ha-spark health` gains a Free Electricity line: disabled, not configured, read failed, not enrolled, no session, or the session times. The new setting `free_electricity_enabled` (default on) switches the source off. Nothing is planned from sessions yet.
