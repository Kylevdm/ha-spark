- **Daily usage bars and a Day-view resolution toggle (#753).** Week, Month
  and Year statistics show one bar per day — house load with import, export,
  solar and the day's cost alongside — and the Year view switches from
  monthly to daily bars. A day with any unmeasured elapsed half-hour is
  marked partial, and the provenance line counts rejected intervals. The Day
  view gains an hourly/half-hourly toggle, hourly by default and remembered
  per device. All time keeps its monthly buckets.
