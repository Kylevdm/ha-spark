# Research: Solis export-limit registers and EPM on the reference firmware

Issue: [#330](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/330), part of [#247](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/247). Date: 2026-10-16.

## Question and scope

What do 43073, 43074 and 33247 mean on the reference RAI-3K-48ES-5G firmware, and can its internal EPM be safely enabled? This is **reading-only research**. The reference site keeps EPM **off**; any trial belongs to the owner at the keypad with supervision, as [#330](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/330) and the [#316 resolution](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316) require.

## Sources and provenance

The ranking follows the [#249 register map](solis-modbus-register-map.md): **(A)** official Solis documents; **(B)** integration source pinned to a commit SHA; **(C)** community reports; **(L)** this repo's live measurements. The [hybrid RS485 protocol PDF](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/) is Solis-authored (A), although hosted by a forum; its revision history ends in 2020 and its accessible register table is for input registers, so it does **not** establish the complete 43073 holding-register layout on this firmware. Solis's [internal EPM guide](https://solis-service.solisinverters.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002317506-internal-epm-set-on-inverters-with-meter-connection), [export-power/failsafe guide](https://usservice.solisinverters.com/support/solutions/articles/73000560491-export-power-set-and-failsafe-configuration), [hybrid zero-export guide](https://solis-service.solisinverters.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002158700-solis-s5-s6-hybrid-inverters-zero-export-set), and [meter/CT technical guide](https://solis-service.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002852865-solis-technical-guide-smart-meter-phase-rotation-and-ct-polarity-verification) are A, but cover several model families.

For B, use [`solis_modbus` switch definitions at `6911f53354b239ba27c4df01569896240022f766`](https://github.com/Pho3niX90/solis_modbus/blob/6911f53354b239ba27c4df01569896240022f766/custom_components/solis_modbus/sensor_data/switch_sensors.py), its [input EPM sensors](https://github.com/Pho3niX90/solis_modbus/blob/6911f53354b239ba27c4df01569896240022f766/custom_components/solis_modbus/sensor_data/hybrid_sensors.py), and [`homeassistant-solax-modbus`'s Solis plugin at `ab73b9e1d154b1db9b98a29f33b49af87af4d191`](https://github.com/wills106/homeassistant-solax-modbus/blob/ab73b9e1d154b1db9b98a29f33b49af87af4d191/custom_components/solax_modbus/plugin_solis.py). The [2022 boards.ie report](https://www.boards.ie/discussion/comment/119711287#Comment_119711287) is C and describes a different hybrid. The [#316 keypad sequence and power trace](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316) are L; they are the only direct evidence for the reference firmware. No device was contacted for this research.

## 1. Units of 43074 and 33247

**Confidence: high for the reference firmware; low for other models.** In the [L sequence](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316), setting *Export Power Set* to 3600 W changed raw 43074 from `0` to `3600` and raw 33247 from `0` to `3600`; both stayed `3600` after EPM was switched off. Thus both are **1 W per count on this firmware**. 33247 is a retained setpoint mirror, not evidence that EPM is active. The earlier [#249 map](solis-modbus-register-map.md#2d-export-limits) said ×100 W, as do the [Solis PDF's 33247 table](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/) and both B implementations ([`solis_modbus` 33247 multiplier](https://github.com/Pho3niX90/solis_modbus/blob/6911f53354b239ba27c4df01569896240022f766/custom_components/solis_modbus/sensor_data/hybrid_sensors.py), [solax 43074 scale](https://github.com/wills106/homeassistant-solax-modbus/blob/ab73b9e1d154b1db9b98a29f33b49af87af4d191/custom_components/solax_modbus/plugin_solis.py)). Those sources **disagree with L**; applying ×100 to raw `3600` would imply 360 kW, which is incompatible with this keypad setting and 3 kW inverter. Solis's [keypad guide](https://usservice.solisinverters.com/support/solutions/articles/73000560491-export-power-set-and-failsafe-configuration) displays backflow settings in W but does not prove Modbus scaling.

What would settle the cross-model question: an exact model/HMI/DSP protocol revision that documents 43074 and 33247, or owner-run keypad changes at two distinct setpoints with simultaneous read-only register captures. Do not generalise the reference scale to another firmware from one sample ([A protocol PDF](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/), [L trace](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316)).

## 2. EPM enable bit in 43073

**Confidence: high for the keypad's bit transition on this firmware; medium for its wider meaning.** The [L sequence](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316) is `6` (off) → `7` (on) → `6` (off). Only bit 0 (`0x0001`) changes, so bit 0 is the keypad *EPM ON/OFF* indication here. Bit 4 stayed clear throughout. [`solis_modbus` pins the feed-in-limit switch to bit 4](https://github.com/Pho3niX90/solis_modbus/blob/6911f53354b239ba27c4df01569896240022f766/custom_components/solis_modbus/sensor_data/switch_sensors.py); that B definition and the [#249 map](solis-modbus-register-map.md#2d-export-limits) **conflict with L**. The [Solis PDF](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/) says 33248 bit 0 reports EPM status but does not establish that holding 43073 has the same layout. A [C report](https://www.boards.ie/discussion/comment/119711287#Comment_119711287) says `43073=48` enabled EPM plus failsafe on another hybrid, showing the model/firmware variance rather than resolving this one.

What would settle the wider mapping: an official 43073 bit table for the reference model and firmware, plus owner-run keypad toggles with 43073 and the read-only status mirrors 33248/33250 captured before and after ([A PDF](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/), [L trace](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316)).

## 3. Bits 1, 3 and 5; should `44` be restored?

**Confidence: medium for bit 5's documented label; low for bits 1 and 3 and for the cause of their change.** `44 = 0x002c` has bits 2, 3 and 5 set; `6 = 0x0006` has bits 1 and 2 set. Bit 5 (`0x0020`) is labelled **FailSafe** in the [Solis hybrid protocol revision history](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/) (revision V002B000D015, which also changes the 33248/33250 status fields). Solis says failsafe stops generation on loss of meter communication in its [EPM instructions](https://solis-service.solisinverters.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002317506-internal-epm-set-on-inverters-with-meter-connection) and [keypad guide](https://usservice.solisinverters.com/support/solutions/articles/73000560491-export-power-set-and-failsafe-configuration). Bit 5 clearing is therefore **consistent with** failsafe being turned off, but that old protocol note alone cannot prove current keypad status. No A or pinned B source found assigns a reliable meaning to **43073** bits 1 or 3 on this firmware. In particular, the [PDF's *33250* bit 1 = meter in grid and bit 3 = parallel-PV CT detection](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/) must **not** be transplanted to *43073*. The change may bundle other keypad settings; the [L trace](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316) does not isolate them.

**Do not restore `44` by a raw write.** It would clear the newly set unknown bit 1 and set the unknown bit 3 and FailSafe bit 5, with no evidence that this recreates a safe configuration. EPM remains off at `6`; the desired failsafe setting should be checked at the keypad by the owner before any EPM trial, because Solis recommends it when export control depends on meter communication ([L state](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316), [A guidance](https://solis-service.solisinverters.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002317506-internal-epm-set-on-inverters-with-meter-connection)). The [#109 flash-endurance research](109-solis-timed-charge-register-flash-endurance.md#write-if-changed-assessment) also supports avoiding speculative or repeated settings writes, without proving 43073 is flash-backed.

What would settle bits 1 and 3: an exact-firmware Solis holding-register table, or an owner-run, one-setting-at-a-time keypad record showing menu label and 43073/33248/33250 before and after each change. A read-only snapshot of current 33248 and 33250 can check status but cannot by itself assign 43073 bits ([A PDF](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/), [L trace](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316)).

## 4. Full-current battery discharge and meter direction/placement

**Confidence: high for the unsafe observed result; low for its root cause or a safe reference configuration.** In [#316's L observation](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316), EPM On with a 3600 W setting coincided with Self-Use discharge at the 62.5 A ceiling and about 2.1 kW of export during 09:41–09:46 UTC on 2026-10-15; switching EPM Off restored normal behaviour by 09:47. This is an observed temporal link, not proof that EPM alone caused the control-loop fault. Solis's [hybrid guide](https://solis-service.solisinverters.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002158700-solis-s5-s6-hybrid-inverters-zero-export-set) warns that a battery can discharge to grid unless zero export is set. The 3600 W setpoint is above this inverter's [3 kW nameplate bound](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316); it cannot function as a tighter device export bound here even if EPM were working correctly.

The inverter meter's **+2.1 kW agrees with actual export**, because Solis defines positive meter active power as power *to* grid and negative as power *from* grid in the [hybrid PDF](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/) and [meter technical guide](https://solis-service.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002852865-solis-technical-guide-smart-meter-phase-rotation-and-ct-polarity-verification). A simple reversed meter sign is therefore **not supported by this snapshot**. Placement remains unverified: Solis says grid-side meter/CT placement must measure the point of common coupling before load and inverter branches, and the selected *Meter in Grid* versus *Meter in Load* mode must match wiring ([A meter guide](https://solis-service.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002852865-solis-technical-guide-smart-meter-phase-rotation-and-ct-polarity-verification), [A EPM guide](https://solis-service.solisinverters.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002317506-internal-epm-set-on-inverters-with-meter-connection)). The [protocol's 33250 status bits](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/) and meter-type/location readback are useful evidence, but a correct sign during export alone cannot prove the meter measures the whole site. No cited evidence establishes that changing a direction or placement menu setting would cure the full-current discharge.

No **validated safe EPM configuration for this reference firmware** follows from these sources. Solis documents an installer path of correct meter placement and mode, appropriate backflow value, meter communications, enabled failsafe, and observed export verification ([A EPM guide](https://solis-service.solisinverters.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002317506-internal-epm-set-on-inverters-with-meter-connection), [A keypad guide](https://usservice.solisinverters.com/support/solutions/articles/73000560491-export-power-set-and-failsafe-configuration), [A zero-export guide](https://solis-service.solisinverters.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002158700-solis-s5-s6-hybrid-inverters-zero-export-set)). That is a **commissioning hypothesis**, not permission to enable EPM unsupervised after this observed behavior. Keep it off at this site ([#330](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/330), [#316](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316)). What would settle the cause is owner/installer verification of wiring and keypad meter mode, known-direction import and export checks against an independent grid measurement, and a supervised owner-run keypad trial with a conservative setting and immediate stop criteria ([A meter guide](https://solis-service.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002852865-solis-technical-guide-smart-meter-phase-rotation-and-ct-polarity-verification), [#330 constraints](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/330)).

## Recommendation for parity and device export bound

- **Parity tier:** Treat 43073 as an opaque bitfield. Any future write must read the current word, change only a firmware-confirmed bit, preserve all other bits, refuse if the read fails, and verify the register and status mirror afterward. On the reference firmware the observed keypad EPM bit is bit 0; do not ship a universal bit-4 or raw `0`/`16` write from the [older map](solis-modbus-register-map.md#2d-export-limits) or [B source](https://github.com/Pho3niX90/solis_modbus/blob/6911f53354b239ba27c4df01569896240022f766/custom_components/solis_modbus/sensor_data/switch_sensors.py). With the current evidence, parity should expose **read-only status** and keep EPM actuation unavailable for this model ([L trace](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316)).
- **Bound read:** Read the device model/rating and EPM state separately. On this site EPM is off and retained `43074 = 33247 = 3600` is **not an active bound**; use the verified **3 kW rated AC output** as the device export bound, subject to the [site export limit and sum rule](../../CONTEXT.md#device-export-bound) and the [#316 two-reading tripwire](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316). For another firmware, an enabled EPM bound needs confirmed enable semantics, correct per-model scaling, readback, and demonstrated enforcement before it can count. A mere 33247 value is insufficient ([L trace](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316), [A protocol](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/)).
- **Restore `44`? No.** Preserve the current off state and seek owner/installer confirmation of the keypad's meter mode and failsafe choice. `44` is an old observation, not a known-good template ([L trace](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316), [A revision history](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/)).

### Open questions for an owner-run, supervised keypad check

1. Which exact meter type/location and failsafe choices appear on the keypad, and do read-only 33248, 33250 and the meter-type/location input agree with them? The [Solis protocol](https://www.photovoltaikforum.com/core/attachment/202315-modbus-solis-rhi-5k-pdf/) defines these readbacks but not this firmware's 43073 bit 1 or 3.
2. Does the inverter meter track independent grid import **and** export, including changes in house load, with the meter/CT physically at the point of common coupling? Solis's [meter guide](https://solis-service.freshdesk.com/en/support/solutions/articles/44002852865-solis-technical-guide-smart-meter-phase-rotation-and-ct-polarity-verification) requires both direction and placement checks.
3. If an owner chooses a supervised keypad trial after those checks, does a conservative export setting hold while Self-Use battery current, inverter meter power and independent site export are observed together, and does switching EPM off promptly restore the baseline? The [#316 trial](https://github.com/Kylevdm/ha-spark/issues/316) failed this behavioral test at 3600 W; no Modbus write or unattended enable is justified.
